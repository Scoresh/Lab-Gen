\section{Race Info}\label{race-info}

\subsection{What is the name of the race?}\label{what-is-the-name-of-the-race}

Seacoast Rotary's 5k Turkey Trot

\subsection{What is the length/distance of the race?}\label{what-is-the-lengthdistance-of-the-race}

5k

\subsection{What is the date and time of the race?}\label{what-is-the-date-and-time-of-the-race}

Thursday, November 27th, at 8:30 AM. This is on Thanksgiving 2025.

\subsection{What is the location of the running course where the race will take place?}\label{what-is-the-location-of-the-running-course-where-the-race-will-take-place}

Downtown Portsmouth NH

\subsection{Describe the running course terrain.}\label{describe-the-running-course-terrain.}

Road Race

\subsection{How large is this event? How many runners typically participate?}\label{how-large-is-this-event-how-many-runners-typically-participate}

This is a fairly large event. In 2024, there were about 2350 people who participated.

\subsection{What is the cost to register for the race?}\label{what-is-the-cost-to-register-for-the-race}

Preregistration is \$30 per adult (20+), \$20 per teenager (13-19), and \$10 per youth. Registration fees are upped starting 10/31/2025 by \$5.

\subsection{Amenities: In addition to your race entry, does the race fee include any other amenities (e.g. a medal, t-shirt, dinner, etc)? If so, specify them.}\label{amenities-in-addition-to-your-race-entry-does-the-race-fee-include-any-other-amenities-e.g.-a-medal-t-shirt-dinner-etc-if-so-specify-them.}

T-shirts and merchandise is NOT provided. Runners get a bib and they will receive a time upon completion.

\subsection{Is this race sponsored for a specific cause (e.g. breast cancer, AIDS, etc)? If so, what?}\label{is-this-race-sponsored-for-a-specific-cause-e.g.-breast-cancer-aids-etc-if-so-what}

Yes; this race is sponsored for the Seacoast Rotary. The Seacoast Rotary sponsors multiple local communities through projects, scholarships, and grants.

\subsection{Is there anything else unique about the race?}\label{is-there-anything-else-unique-about-the-race}

This race takes place on Thanksgiving; hence, it is called a "turkey trot." This race has occured every year in Portsmouth NH. I have had many friends who have competed, but so far, my friends have not won it.

\subsection{Is this a race you would ever consider running? Why or why not?}\label{is-this-a-race-you-would-ever-consider-running-why-or-why-not}

This race is one that I would never consider running. The Turkey Trot occurs in an interesting time for me; I just finished the Cross Country season and am getting into Swim Team shape. I do not want to overexert myself by both running AND getting into swim shape because I do not have the time for it. If the Turkey Trot, or a similar race, occured a week or so after XC states, I would be more than happy to run.

\subsection{In one paragraph, explain the specific steps you would need to take to prepare for this race.}\label{in-one-paragraph-explain-the-specific-steps-you-would-need-to-take-to-prepare-for-this-race.}

To prepare for the Turkey Trot, you first have to check your fitness level. If you ran in the fall, then this following plan should work. A month or two in advance, begin working on your speed work. A 5k is a distance race, but it is also a sprint. There should be virtually no point in your 5k where you feel good. Begin building your mileage week by week and make sure to warm up, cool down, and stretch. This will keep you in optimal shape for the race. A week before the race, begin tapering out. Take slower runs and get yourself in the zone. Pay attention to what you eat; eat what works best for you. The night before, eat up on some carbohydrates to get some sugar in your body to burn. Day of the race, get to the venue two hours in advance and walk the course. Get familiar with the turns and potential choke points. Then, warm up by doing a mile or two and dynamic stretches. You should now be ready for the race. Make sure to have your bib and light sippage of water before and after. Now, you are ready for the race! Have a great race and do not worry about other people; only YOUR time matters.

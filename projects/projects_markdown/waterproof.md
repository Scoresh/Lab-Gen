### Manufactoring Questions
A manufacturer of boots plans to conduct an experiment to compare a new method of waterproofing to the current method.  The appearance of the boots is not changed by either method.  One hundred people who live in Seattle, where it rains frequently, volunteer to participate in the study.   They will wear the boots as they normally would for 6 months.  At the end of the 6 months, the boots will be returned to the company to be evaluated for water damage.

(a) Identify the following:  

1) The explanatory variable.  
    + Old versus. New Waterproofing Method
2) The response variable.  
    + Difference in Water Damage
3) The experimental units.
    + Subjects: People who live in Seattle participating in the study

(b) Identify some possibly confounding variables.

(c) Describe a design for this experiment that uses the 100 volunteers.  Include commentary on how it would be implemented.  Recall, there are three experimental designs in the Great Bay Stat curriculum: 

+ Completely randomized design  
+ Randomized block design  
+ Matched pairs design

(d) Three of the four principles of experimental design are necessary for any well-designed experiment: control, randomization, replication.  For the design that you chose in part charlie, describe the why and the how you would implement each of the three principles.

(e) Could your design be double blinded?  Explain.

(f) Could you generalize the results of this study to customers who purchase these boots from across the U.S.?  Explain.

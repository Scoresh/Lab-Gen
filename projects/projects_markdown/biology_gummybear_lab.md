# Elements & Hypothesis

## Hypothesis

If gummy bears are submerged into solutions of oil, water, vinegar, and salt water, the gummy bear submerged in normal water will expand the most in comparison to pre-treatment levels. 


## Experimental Elements
- Independent Variable: Solution that the gummy bears are exposed to
- Dependent Variable: Ratio of weight gained ($\frac{Weight_FINAL}{Weight_INITIAL}$)
- Experimental Groups:
    - Group 1: Gummy bear exposed to Water ($H_2 O$) 
    - Group 2: Gummy bear exposed to Salt Water ($H_2 O + NaCl$)
    - Group 3: Gummy bear exposed to Oil 
    - Group 4: Gummy bear exposed to Vinegar
- Negative Control Group:
    - Gummy Bear exposed to NO solution
- Controls
    - Time spent in each solution
    - Weighing methods
    - Volume of each individual solution

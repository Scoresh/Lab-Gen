#### 1. Why do we sample?  Oftentimes the purpose is to estimate unknown/unknowable population parameters like  the population mean.  With that in mind, order the Sample Designs from best to worst in terms of which design tended to better estimate the actual population mean corn yield. 

Since the population mean looks to be between 200 and 250, three of the five distributions fit this description. The SRS is roughly unimodal, with the greatest (out of the three tables with a mean ~225) inter-quartile range. The best out of the three in terms of the IQR is the "Stratified by Column", and the second best seems to be "Stratified by Row." This is because the column has a significantly lower IQR than row. Convenience 1 & 2 were inherently flawed in their sampling methods, so a mean of 150 (convenience 1), and a mean of 175 (convenience 2) are not correct in terms of results. In this case, the order will be (best to worst): {Stratified by Column, Stratified by Row, SRS, Convenience 2, Convenience 1}

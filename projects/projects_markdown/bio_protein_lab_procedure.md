# Purpose
To show the denaturing of amylase through the change in pH levels and how the efficacy and efficiency of enzyme performance are scale-based.

# Materials
- Vinegar (changed from citric acid for efficiency purposes)
- Water (distilled)
- Human spit/saliva (for amylase enzyme)
- Glucose Strip
- Saltine Crackers
- 4 Test tubes

# Preparations
Be sure to have test tubes labeled with a neutral pH (this is your control group), a 6.0, a 5.0, and a 4.0. This is where each solution will be stored. Also at this time, gather 4 crackers and place them in respect to a test tube. Next to each of these crackers, be sure to have a glucose strip on hand, ready to collect when the solution interacts with the Saltines.
